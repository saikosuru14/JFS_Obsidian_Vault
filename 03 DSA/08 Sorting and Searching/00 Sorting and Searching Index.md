# Sorting and Searching

> Module index - part of the [[DSA Index|DSA]] learning path.

## Overview
Sorting organizes data (often a prerequisite for other techniques); searching finds elements — and on sorted data, **binary search** makes it O(log n). Together they're the most fundamental algorithmic pair, and "sort first" unlocks two pointers, greedy, and dedup.
