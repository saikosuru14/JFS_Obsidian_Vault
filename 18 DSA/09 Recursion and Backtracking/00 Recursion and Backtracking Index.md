# Recursion and Backtracking

> Module index - part of the [[DSA Index|DSA]] learning path.

## Overview
Recursion solves a problem by solving smaller instances of itself. **Backtracking** is recursion that explores choices and undoes them to enumerate all valid configurations. **Divide and conquer** splits, solves independently, and combines. Together they cover permutations, combinations, subsets, and the recursive core of trees, graphs, and DP.

## Branch (expand)
- **[[Recursion]]** — base case + recursive case; the call stack.
  - [[Backtracking]] — choose / explore / un-choose over a decision tree.
  - [[Divide and Conquer]] — split, solve, combine.
