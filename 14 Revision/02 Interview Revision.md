## Must-Know by Area
- **Java:** HashMap internals, equals/hashCode, concurrency utilities, GC.
- **Spring:** IoC/DI, bean scopes, request lifecycle, `@Transactional`, JPA N+1.
- **Databases:** isolation levels, indexing, normalization, SQL vs NoSQL.
- **Microservices:** resilience patterns, Saga, idempotency.
- **Kafka:** partitions/ordering, consumer groups, delivery semantics.
- **System Design:** requirements → estimation → components → trade-offs.
