# System Design

> Domain hub — principles, building blocks, and case studies for large-scale systems.

## Overview
System design is about making trade-offs to meet scale, availability, and consistency requirements. This domain covers the fundamentals, the reusable building blocks, data-management strategies, distributed patterns, and worked case studies.

## Learning Roadmap
1. [[SD Fundamentals Index|SD Fundamentals]] — scalability, availability, reliability, latency vs throughput.
2. [[Building Blocks Index|Building Blocks]] — load balancing, caching, CDN, queues, API design.
3. [[Data Management Index|Data Management]] — replication, sharding, partitioning, CAP, consistency.
4. [[Distributed Patterns Index|Distributed Patterns]] — CQRS, event sourcing, distributed locking, idempotency.
5. [[Case Studies Index|Case Studies]] — URL shortener, chat, news feed, payments, ride sharing, and more.

## Suggested Study Order
Master the fundamentals and vocabulary, then the building blocks you'll reuse in every design, then data-management and distributed patterns, and finally practice with case studies under time pressure.

## Prerequisites
- [[Computer Networks]], [[Database Fundamentals]], [[Caching]] basics.
- [[Microservices]] and [[Apache Kafka]] help for distributed topics.

## Related Concepts
- [[High-Level Design]] and [[Low-Level Design]] in [[Interview]] prep.

## Interview Focus
- Drive requirements and capacity estimation first.
- Justify data store and partitioning choices.
- Discuss caching, load balancing, and consistency trade-offs.
- Walk a case study end to end (e.g., [[URL Shortener]]).
