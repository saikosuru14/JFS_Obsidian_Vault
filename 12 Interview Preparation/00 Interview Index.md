# Interview Preparation

> Domain hub — Q&A, question banks, HLD/LLD, and behavioral prep.

## Overview
This domain consolidates interview-focused material: rapid-recall Q&A, per-technology question banks, high-level and low-level design practice, and behavioral preparation.

## Learning Roadmap
1. [[Technical Q&A Index|Technical Q&A]] — Java, Spring, Hibernate, Microservices, Kafka.
2. [[Interview Question Banks Index|Interview Question Banks]] — per-technology question sets.
3. [[High-Level Design Index|High-Level Design]] — HLD strategy, capacity, caching, scaling.
4. [[Low-Level Design Index|Low-Level Design]] — SOLID, patterns, UML, classic LLD problems.
5. [[Behavioral Index|Behavioral]] — HR questions and coding-interview strategy.

## Suggested Study Order
Use Q&A and question banks for breadth and recall, then drill HLD and LLD with the strategy notes and worked problems, and prepare behavioral answers last.

## Prerequisites
- Core domains: [[Java]], [[Spring]], [[Microservices]], [[System Design]].

## Related Concepts
- [[System Design]] — the HLD module builds directly on it.
- [[Object-Oriented Programming]] and [[SOLID Principles]] — the basis for LLD.

## How to Use
- Time-box mock questions.
- For design problems, always start with [[Requirement Gathering]] and [[Capacity Estimation]].
- Track weak areas with the `status: Need Revision` property and the Revision base.
