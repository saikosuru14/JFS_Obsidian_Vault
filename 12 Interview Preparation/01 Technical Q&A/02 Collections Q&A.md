# Collections Q&A

## Q: ArrayList vs LinkedList?
A: ArrayList offers fast random access; LinkedList is efficient for insertions/removals in the middle.

## Q: HashMap vs ConcurrentHashMap?
A: ConcurrentHashMap supports concurrent access without synchronizing the entire map.
